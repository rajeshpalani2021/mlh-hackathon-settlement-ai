# US T+1 and cutoffs

- US equities settle **T+1** (one business day after trade date) since May 2024.
- **Affirmation cutoff: 9:00 PM ET on trade date.** Allocations and confirmations should be
  affirmed by then to settle on time. T+1 leaves only hours to fix a broken instruction.
- **Trade-date boundary for overnight trading: 8:00 PM ET.** Executions at or after 8:00 PM ET
  are booked to the **next business day's** trade date, and settle one business day after that.
- Weekends and market holidays are skipped for both trade and settle dates.
